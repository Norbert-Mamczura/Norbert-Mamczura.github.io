# Events

## Folkowisko

From 2023 I coordinated the festival’s entire sound production.

I repeatedly completed training in mass-event safety and emergency response, organised by the head of security at Pol’and’Rock Festival.

I coordinated volunteers, external companies and musicians, including Kapela ze Wsi Warszawa, Żywiołak and Nicponie.

I operated equipment and provided sound for stages and other festival areas, mainly using Soundcraft Ui16 and Allen & Heath SQ-5 consoles.

**Result:** I reduced delays on the main stage from the usual four hours to seven minutes in my first year, and brought them down to zero minutes in subsequent years.

**Website:** I created a system for publishing workshop information (WordPress, Google Apps Script) and displaying it to participants at the event through Starlink.

## Cieszanów Rock Festival

In 2020, I worked on organising the entire festival during the pandemic, including planning and communication with media and bands.

In 2021, I managed the entire small stage: coordinating teams, volunteers and employees, promoting this part of the event, and working with bands, musicians and external companies.

In 2022 and 2023, I worked on the main stage with artists including KSU and Kazik.

## Additional music experience

I organised and conducted recording sessions for bands and vocalists, and mixed and mastered their recordings.

I have played guitar, bass and drums extensively and have performed more than 100 concerts.
