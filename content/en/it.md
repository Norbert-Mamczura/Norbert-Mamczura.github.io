## Commercial projects

**Event management system**
*Django, HTMX*
An extensive CRM system written in Django for managing employees and companies across multiple events at the same time. It allows organisers to aggregate subcontractor data, manage access and wristbands, and lets security staff identify workers through a QR-code system.
The system was successfully used at major mass events, including Ed Sheeran’s concert in Wrocław and Drift Masters at the National Stadium in Warsaw.

**Call-centre conversation analysis system**
*Whisper API, LLM (RAG), Django, Celery, Redis*
An application designed to analyse call-centre conversations and evaluate consultants based on audio transcriptions and LLM-generated assessments. It was initially developed as an internal startup project and was later sold.

I recently created version 2.0 using AI capabilities that had not previously been available: Structured Outputs and Polish-language speaker diarisation. To reduce dependencies, I also replaced Redis and Celery with my own implementations.

**Text-to-speech application for an Emergency Management Centre**
*Google Cloud text-to-speech, Django*
A system used to send voice announcements to residents through voice mailing. Speech is generated through the Google Cloud Platform API. I developed this component and integrated it with the company’s existing systems.

**Database sales website**
*Django, DRF*
A sales website featuring several interesting ways of presenting data. Its most distinctive element is the separation of the server-side application and frontend between two different servers (PythonAnywhere and nazwa.pl), connected through an API. This gives the client access to modern technologies while avoiding a complicated CNAME redirection structure.

**Company website management**
*WordPress, HTML, CSS, JS*
I created, modified and managed websites built with WordPress, Django and traditional code.

**Folkowisko festival website**
I created a system for publishing workshop information (WordPress, Google Apps Script) and displaying it to festival participants through a Starlink connection.
